# Motion Life Support: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

Motion Life Support ("Mo") is the AI support and moderation bot for the Motion Life Discord server. It is run
by the Motion Life server owner (Discord: scar.hq). Mo only works inside the
Motion Life server and in DMs with the server owner.

## What Mo reads
- **Support tickets:** messages and screenshots you post in a private ticket you open.
- **The help channel:** questions you post there, so Mo can answer FiveM / PC setup questions.
- **Public chat channels:** message text, only to catch spam, scam posts and hacked-account posts.
- **Messages the server owner forwards or links to Mo.**

Mo does not read the staff category, does not track presence, and does not scan member lists.

## What we keep, and for how long
- **Public chat:** not stored. The spam check holds the last 15 minutes in memory, then drops it.
- **Removed scam posts:** a masked copy (links and handles blanked) for 90 days, so Mo spots the same scam again.
- **Tickets:** stored while open, with keys, emails and order numbers masked first. Deleted 30 days after the
  ticket closes, except your most recent ticket, kept 90 days so we remember your issue, then deleted.
- **Screenshots:** we keep a short text description, not the image.
- **Orders:** to check a purchase, Mo looks up the order in our store system. We don't copy payment details.

## Who sees it
- The server owner. Data sits in a private database only the owner can access.
- Our AI provider receives message text and screenshots only to write Mo's reply or describe the image.
- Discord, as the platform the server runs on.

We **do not** sell your data, share it for ads, or use your messages to train AI models.

## Your choices
- **Delete your data:** open a support ticket in the Motion Life server and ask, or DM scar.hq on Discord.
  We delete your stored tickets, notes and examples and confirm in the ticket.
- **Talk to a human:** ask in your ticket and the owner takes over.
- Mo is an AI. It is labelled with the APP tag and says so.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we update the date above and post it in the server.

## Contact
Discord: scar.hq, or a support ticket in the Motion Life server.
